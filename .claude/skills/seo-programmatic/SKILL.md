# Programmatic pages on aeternawealth.com

Read first: `AGENTS.md` (site messaging, publishing protocol), `docs/seo/strategy.md`, the last entries of
`docs/seo/log.md`. This skill sets the quality bar; `seo-daily` is the routine that ships the work.

## Where we stand (update when it changes)
- About 3,950 pages built, about 3,640 in the sitemap. Page types and their generators:
  fund pages and category pages (`site/build_site.py`), fund houses and comparisons (`site/build_pages.py`),
  stock-holding pages `/mutual-funds/holding/<stock>/` (`site/stock_pages.py`), market data (`site/market_pages.py`),
  top lists (`site/top_pages.py`), tax (`site/tax_pages.py`), guides (`site/learn_pages.py`).
- Search Console (26 Nov 2026): about 1,130 pages "Discovered - currently not indexed". **Google is rationing this
  site.** More pages now dilutes it further. The lever is making existing pages better and more trusted, not more.

## The gate for a new page type (all must be yes; write the answers in the commit message)
1. **Real search demand**: people type this (Search Console query, Google autocomplete or "People also ask").
   Record the target query in `docs/seo/keywords.md`.
2. **Our own data answers it better** than the current top results: a number or table nobody else publishes free
   (holdings changes, ratings vs own index, flows, crowding). Rewording AMFI's scheme list is not enough.
3. **Every page differs in substance**: at least 3 data points that change page to page, plus a plain-English
   reading generated from them (see "Answer first"). If two pages would read the same with the name swapped, merge them.
4. **A minimum-data floor**: no page when the data is thin (e.g. a stock held by fewer than N schemes, a fund with no
   rating and no portfolio). Below the floor: no page, or `noindex,follow` and left out of the sitemap.
5. **Linked from strong pages**: the hub, related pages and at least one high-traffic page link to it. No orphans.
6. **Fits the site's messaging**: no "best"/"buy"/"top picks" claims, no cost verdicts, no index-vs-index or
   Direct-vs-Regular pages that work against the business, nothing that ranks or judges funds on expense ratio.

Roll out in batches: first the 50-100 pages with the most data and demand, wait 4-6 weeks for impressions in Search
Console, then extend. Never publish thousands of new URLs in one deploy.

## Template rules (every programmatic page)
- **Answer first.** The first paragraph answers the search in a sentence built from the numbers
  ("376 schemes hold Axis Bank, worth about ₹82,692 cr; 2 added it last month, 4 sold out"). Generated by code
  from the data, never by an LLM per page.
- **Title**: what people type + the key number + the month, under 65 characters (`fit_title` trims; check
  `tools/seo_audit.py` I1). Old fund names stay in the title in short form (`search_name`).
- **Description**: the page's own numbers in one or two sentences, under 158 characters (`fit_desc`).
- **One H1**, matching the title's intent. Breadcrumb JSON-LD on every page (`crumbs`).
- **Structured data only where true**: FAQPage only for questions answered visibly on the page; Dataset for
  data tables; no Review/AggregateRating (ratings are ours, not reviews, and self-serving review markup is against
  Google's rules).
- **Data date visible** on every page, and the trust box (`trust_box` in `site/build_site.py`) present on data and
  tax pages: who publishes, ARN with AMFI's search link, sources, data date, links to Method, Editorial policy,
  Disclosures, About.
- **Internal links**: each page links to its hub, 3-8 related pages (same category, same stock's top holders,
  same fund house), and the method page. Use descriptive anchor text, not "click here".
- **Regular and Direct pages** are separate funds to Google: each leads with what is specific to its plan.
- **Canonical** is always the page itself; near-duplicates are merged with a 301 in `_redirects`, not canonicalised away.
- **Speed**: page under 100 KB compressed, no layout shift, no client-only content (Google must see the numbers in
  the HTML).

## Pruning
A page type or page with no impressions after 3 months: improve it once (title, summary, links), then
`noindex,follow` (still useful to visitors) or merge with a redirect. Pruning is logged in `docs/seo/log.md` with the
Search Console evidence. Current noindex set: `NOINDEX_PATHS` in `site/build_site.py` plus the less-compared
comparison pairs (`site/build_pages.py`).

## Ideas that pass the gate (backlog candidates; check demand first)
- Stock pages: "mutual funds that bought <stock> in <month>" sections, not separate pages.
- Fund-house pages: what this fund house bought and sold this month (from holdings), one section per AMC page.
- Category pages: "funds in <category> that beat <index> over 3 years" as a visible, sortable table.
- Fund-manager pages only once manager data is tracked reliably (`AGENTS.md`: not yet automated).

## Never
Pages generated per keyword with spun text, doorway pages (city x product, "best fund for <profession>"),
pages for searches we cannot answer with our own data, scraped content, hidden text, cloaking, or switching
`noindex` off to inflate page counts. These are Google spam-policy violations and breach AMFI/SEBI advertising rules.

## Before publishing
`python site/build_site.py`, `python tools/seo_audit.py`, `python tools/audit.py` (no FAIL), the tests in
`.github/workflows/tests.yml`, and a Playwright look at 2-3 sample pages of each changed type at 390px and 1280px,
light and dark. Then publish per `AGENTS.md`.
