# Thesis: rating PMS strategies (draft for the owner's approval, 27 Nov 2026)

## What is rated
- Source: APMI's monthly investment-approach reports (every PMS strategy, as SEBI requires): assets and time-weighted
  returns after all fees, before tax. Complete from April 2023; 43 monthly reports to October 2026.
- Shown: strategies with Rs 100 crore or more (kept until below Rs 75 crore, so pages do not flicker). 548 of 1,851.
- Rated: discretionary only, with a 3-year return and at least 24 months of monthly record: 321 (equity 283,
  debt 12, hybrid and multi-asset 26).
- Not rated, listed on the manager's page with the reason: non-discretionary (each client approves trades, so the
  return depends on the clients), under 3 years, liquid strategies (cash parking), and strategies filed as equity
  that do not behave like stock portfolios (beta to Nifty 500 under 0.5, or arbitrage / unlisted / pre-IPO /
  special situations by name). These are data rules, not judgements of the manager.

## Yardstick
Nifty 500 TRI month-end to month-end, the same months as the strategy. It tracks BSE 500 TRI (APMI's displayed
index) within a few tenths of a percent (1 year to Oct 2026: -6.8% vs -7.2%). Debt, hybrid and multi-asset strategies
are compared with their peers.

## Score (percentile within the peer group, weights)
Equity: 3-year return over the index 20; information ratio 20; share of rolling 12-month spells ahead of the index
15; down capture (lower is better) 15; worst fall 10; worst 12 months against the index 10; share of months ahead 10.
Debt / hybrid and multi-asset: annualised return 30; share of rolling 12-month spells ahead of the peer median 20;
worst fall 20; volatility (lower is better) 15; worst 12 months 15.

## Tiers (same shape as the mutual fund tiers)
A+ (Leading): top 10% and score 70+ and, for equity, a positive 3-year return over the index. A (Strong): top 30%
and 55+, and for equity a positive 3-year excess. C (Lagging): bottom 30%. B (In line): the rest.
Money-flow guard: a strategy whose clients withdrew more than 30% of its assets in the last 12 months (estimated:
change in assets not explained by returns) is held at B, with the reason shown.
Result (Oct 2026): equity A+ 28, A 54, B 116, C 85.

## Why these choices
- PMS sites rank by trailing 1-year return; the top of that list today is strategies under 2 years old. Requiring 3
  years and weighting consistency and falls rewards a record, not a lucky year.
- Rolling 12-month spells and down capture separate managers who beat the index steadily from those who beat it in
  one rally.
- Information ratio is the core skill measure (excess return per unit of tracking error).

## Known limits (stated on the method page)
- 43 months: one long rise and one fall. Survivorship: strategies that shrank or closed are not in the pool, so
  medians flatter the industry.
- No holdings, fees or cap-size labels in APMI data: equity is one peer group (large, multi, mid and small cap
  together). Option for the owner: split by style using betas to Nifty 50 / Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250 (data-driven,
  no labels needed). Recommended for v2 once a year of ratings exists.
- Returns are the strategy's; a client's own return differs with timing and fee plan.
