How a rating is built

Measured against the category's own index, then against peers

A fund is compared only with funds in its SEBI category and plan type, using the total-return version of the category's benchmark. Each measure becomes a percentile among peers, and the weighted result sets the tier.

We tested our own ratings

Last year's top-rated funds rarely stayed on top

We rebuilt our ratings at 13 dates from 2019 to 2025 using only data available at the time, then checked what happened next. Top-rated funds beat their index over the following three years less often than the average fund. Rankings by trailing returns did no better.

So a rating is where the work starts, not where it ends. We choose on cost, overlap, mandate, fit with your other holdings and tax, and review the choice every year. Our tiers describe a fund's track record; they are not forecasts.
