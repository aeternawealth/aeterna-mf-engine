About Regular plans

These SIPs are in Regular plans. Aeterna is paid a commission by the fund house, included in the plan's expense ratio. After a year of SIPs that is about ₹4,301 a year more than the Direct plans of the same funds, and it grows with the balance. Direct plans have no commission and no distributor service.

What we watch, and when we look again

  • Every month (after ratings update on the 11th and 16th): any fund held falling to C, a mandate breach, a fund manager change or a merger.
  • Any sleeve more than 5% points (or 25%) away from its target, checked at each review and after any 20% market move. New money corrects it first.
  • The glide path: from 2041, money moves from equity to debt or arbitrage each year so that a fall close to the goal does not derail it.
  • Next full review: November 2027, or sooner if income, goals or plans change.
